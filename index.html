Writing notation

Mark each passage with [SWARA] and [SAHITYA] rows, optionally preceded by [SECTION: Pallavi]. Only the swara row makes sound; the sahitya row is text and is highlighted in step with the music.

[SECTION: Pallavi] [SWARA] G,,, M, G, M,M, [MGM,]N, [SAHITYA] go vard dha na gi ri

Symbols

Gone note-space, pitch resolved through the selected raga

Keyboard

Space play or pause · S stop · R restart · M metronome · ← → tempo by 2 BPM. Every control is reachable by touch as well.

Timing

secondsPerBeat = 60 ÷ BPM. secondsPerNoteSpace = secondsPerBeat ÷ note-spaces per beat. At 60 BPM with 4 note-spaces to the beat, one note-space is 0.25 s and a bracketed swara is 0.125 s.
